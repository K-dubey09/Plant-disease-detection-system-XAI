# AI-Assisted Development Disclosure

LeafGuard is an undergraduate research and engineering project by **Kushagra Dubey**. In the
interest of an honest accounting of how it was built, this document discloses the use of AI coding
assistants (large language models) during development.

## What AI assistance was used for

AI coding assistants were used for **routine scaffolding and acceleration**, including:

- First-draft Jetpack Compose layouts and boilerplate UI code.
- Gradle and CI configuration scaffolding.
- Conversion-script skeletons (`models/conversion/convert.py`).
- First-draft prose for documentation (README, architecture notes, the project report).
- Exploratory code searches across the codebase.

All material generated by an assistant was **reviewed, revised, tested, and integrated by the
author**. The author is responsible for every line committed to this repository.

## What is the author's own work

The substantive intellectual work is the author's own and was **not** generated by an assistant:

- The **model architecture and domain-adaptation recipe** — the DINOv2 ViT-S/14 backbone, the
  gradient-reversal domain-adversarial head, the progressive-unfreeze schedule, the multi-crop
  consistency regularization, and the field-oversampling strategy.
- The **experimental methodology** and the seven-iteration development arc with its diagnosed
  failures (11% → 49% → 34% → 67.3% → 86.7%).
- The **ablation analysis** isolating each component's contribution.
- The **on-device inference, integrity, and explainability design** — the TFLite inference path,
  the SHA-256 model-integrity guard, and the occlusion-sensitivity heatmap implementation.
- All **engineering and validation decisions**, and the verification of the numerical-equivalence
  and field-accuracy results.

## Why this disclosure exists

This disclosure is made so that the project is **not presented as if every implementation detail
were manually typed**, while making clear that the design, the methodology, and the validation are
the author's responsibility. The same disclosure is surfaced:

- In the Android application, on the **About** screen (v2.6.0.0 onward).
- In the project report (`docs/IEEE_format/LeafGuard_IEEE_Project_Report.pdf`), in the
  "AI-Assisted Development Disclosure" section.

The research paper is withheld from this public repository until publication, per standard
pre-publication practice, and contains no credentials or access tokens.
